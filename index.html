How the System Works

A fully managed tracking layout engineered to absorb campus administrative friction.

01
Student Logs Complaint

Student calls the phone helpline or enters it on the website portal.

02
Operator Registers Ticket

Our live helpline operator screens out spam and enters details into the system.

03
System Auto-Forwards

The complaint instantly drops to the concerned staff member via SMS routing.

04
Officer Closes Issue

The staff in-charge views details and closes the complaint on the Mobile App.
